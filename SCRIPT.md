# LUNCH MONEY, Script & Storyboard

**Format:** Four-panel comic (2×2 page) + hosted web page + square share card
**Track:** BCH BLAZE 2026, Media Track (comics and AI-created/assisted art are explicitly eligible)
**Author:** dunoil (@dunoil), solo entry, created during the hackathon window (19 Nov 2026)
**Logline:** A food-truck vendor's supplier payment is stuck in the banking system minutes before the lunch rush, until a customer shows him money that moves at the speed of lunch.

## Characters (consistent across panels)
- **Rafi**, young food-truck vendor, short black hair, green apron over a white t-shirt.
- **Maya**, regular customer, ponytail, yellow jacket.
- **The supplier**, bearded man in a cap and blue work shirt, carrying vegetable crates.

## Panel script

**Panel 1, The Problem (top left)**
*Visual:* Rafi stares anxiously at his phone beside his orange food truck; a wall clock reads almost noon; a long queue of hungry customers stretches behind him.
*Caption:* "11:40 AM. The lunch queue is forming, but Rafi's supplier is still waiting on yesterday's bank transfer. No payment, no ingredients. No ingredients, no lunch."

**Panel 2, The Demo (top right)**
*Visual:* Maya leans to the truck window holding up her phone, showing a green wallet screen with a QR code; Rafi looks up, surprised and hopeful.
*Caption:* "A regular spots the panic. 'Forget the bank queue,' she says. 'Pay the way I'd pay you, Bitcoin Cash.' One scan of the QR code…"

**Panel 3, The Transfer (bottom left)**
*Visual:* Close-up: two smartphones on a dark teal background, a glowing green coin with a bold white "B" arcing between the screens in a trail of light.
*Caption:* "…and it's already there. Seconds, not days, and the fee is less than a penny. No account to open. No permission to ask. Phone to phone, like handing over cash."

**Panel 4, The Payoff (bottom right)**
*Visual:* Golden afternoon: Rafi happily serves the crowd; the supplier delivers crates of fresh vegetables; Maya eats at the window; a green QR sign is taped to the truck.
*Caption:* "By noon the supplier is paid, the crates are in, and the whole queue eats. Rafi tapes a QR code to the window. Money that moves at the speed of lunch."

## Educational payload (page's facts section)
1. Wallet-to-wallet BCH payments are effectively instant for everyday purchases (0-conf), Bitcoin Cash Podcast FAQ.
2. Typical network fees are under $0.01, bitcoincash.org.
3. No bank account or company sign-up needed to send/receive, just a wallet app, bitcoincash.org.
4. Fixed supply of 21 million BCH, bitcoincash.org.
5. CashTokens launched 2023; ABLA (May 2024) makes the block-size limit adaptive with a guaranteed 32 MB minimum, bitcoincash.org / BCH Podcast FAQ.

No statistics were invented for this piece; every factual claim traces to the two sources above.
